01 / FRONTEND PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

One blueprint.
A world of possibilities.

Product Center & Bundles Workbench turned multi-team configuration requests into reusable, self-service workflows for financial institutions.

20+ institutions100+ actively managed financial products
Vue 3Module FederationSchema Builder
Read the engineering story ＋

We defined the blueprint-driven architecture for templates, input flows, validation, and bundle creation. Together, we delivered HDFC UAT, role-based authorization, issuance tracking, Bundle Details, and operations workflows.

We built modular frontends with Vue 3 and Module Federation, supported by Nginx, CDN and edge caching, multi-region deployments, automated regression suites, and controls for bi-monthly production releases.

02 / AI PRODUCT ENGINEERING

Intelligent by design.
Human at the heart.

An AI-assisted configuration product to create, update, and validate banking bundles across cards, payments, accounts, wallets, notifications, and policies.

3–4 weeks → under 7 daysConfiguration turnaround, with 98% correctness
AI guardrailsHuman approvalFull-stack delivery
Read the engineering story ＋

We architected and deployed the product with request clarification, approved-template constraints, schema and rule validation, explicit user approval, role-based OAuth authorization, and compliance checks before enterprise changes.

Our team of 10 engineers collaborated through architecture and code reviews, mentoring, and hands-on technical guidance. We coordinated delivery closely with product, security, and business teams.
